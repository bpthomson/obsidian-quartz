| --------------- | :-----: | :-------: | :----: |
| 翻譯              |   建議    |    推薦     |   指引   |
| 語氣              |   輕微    |     中     |  專業權威  |
| 接 Ving          |    ✅    |     ✅     |   ✅    |
| 接 that + S + 原V |    ✅    |     ✅     |   ✅    |
| 接 Noun          |    ✅    |     ✅     |   ✅    |
| 接 人 + to V      |    ❌    |     ✅     |   ✅    |
| 接 to V          |    ❌    |     ❌     |   ❌    |
| 以物品作主詞          |    ✅    |     ✅     |   ❌    |

> [!Note]
> 習慣都接 Ving 和 that + S + (should) + 原V 即可
## 1. Suggest
- **提議、建議、指出 (語氣輕微)**
- 用法
	1. + V-ing
	2. + that + S + (should) + 原V
	3. + Noun
- *I suggest that we smash a pie in his face on his birthday.*
- *The study suggests that every person has been smashed by a pie at least once in his life.*

> [!warning]
> `Suggest` 不可接 `人 + to V`，也不可直接 `to V`
> ❌ *I suggest he to bring a raincoat on his birthday.*

## 2. Recommend
- **推薦、背書 (信心推薦)**
- 用法
	1. + V-ing
	2. + that + S + (should) + 原V
	3. + Noun
	4. + 人 + to V
- *I recommend Dan to be the team leader.*
- *The travel guide recommends visiting the market early.*

> [!Warning]
> `Recommend`, `Advise` 不可直接 `to V`，一定要 `人 + to V`
> ❌ *The travel guide recommends to visit the market early.*

## 3. Advise
- **忠告、指引 (專業指示)**
- 用法
	1. + V-ing
	2. + that + S + (should) + 原V
	3. + Noun
	4. + 人 + to V
- *The doctor advised Grandpa to exercise everyday.*

> [!Warning]
> `Advise` 不可以物品當主詞，其強調「人對人發出權威性的忠告」
> ❌ *The textbook advises that we heat up the solution by an alcohol lamp.*
