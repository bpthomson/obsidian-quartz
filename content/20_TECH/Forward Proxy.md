## An intermediary that acts on behalf of the clients, hiding their identities.

![[20_TECH-Forward Proxy.webp]]
#### Advantages
- `Prevent cyberattacks` : Client IP is not visible on the Internet
- `Filtering site` : Block certain websites when using a school IP
- `Cache` : Cache the contents
